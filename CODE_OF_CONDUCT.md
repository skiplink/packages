# Code of conduct

Skiplink's issue trackers are for getting accessibility tooling to work. Everyone is
welcome here, whatever their experience, background or the assistive technology they
use.

**Expected:** be specific, be patient, assume good faith, and remember that the person
on the other side may be reading your words through a screen reader at 2× speed — say
what you mean plainly.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or jokes,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of a discussion.
Mocking someone's disability, or their accessibility needs, is not tolerated in any
form.

**Enforcement.** Comments that break this are edited or removed, and repeat or serious
cases lead to a block. Report a problem privately to **hello@skiplink.dev**; reports are
read by the maintainer and kept confidential.

This code is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
